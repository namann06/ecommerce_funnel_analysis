🎯 Conversion Optimization & Revenue Recovery Strategy

1. Checkout Abandonment Recovery (Biggest Revenue Leakage)

Diagnostic Finding: 69.35% of users who reach the Checkout stage drop off before completing their purchase (2,444 abandoned carts).
Solution: Implement dynamic 2-hour automated cart abandonment emails with single-click checkout links and personalized product incentives.

2. Channel ROI & Ad Spend Target Allocation

Diagnostic Finding: Google Ads ($312.8K) and Organic ($307.4K) yield the highest per-session value ($122+), while Email ($276.1K) has the lowest conversion rate (10.20%).
Solution: Reallocate 15% of underperforming ad spend into retargeting email leads with tailored landing pages and VIP buyer discounts.
